# Machine Learning Interview Corpus

## Math & Statistics Foundations
Cover vectors, matrices, derivatives, probability, distributions, expectation, estimation, and hypothesis testing.
## Data Preparation
Handle missing data, categories, scaling, outliers, splitting, imbalance, and leakage prevention.
## EDA & Feature Engineering
Explore distributions and relationships and construct informative, stable, explainable features.
## Linear Regression
Understand objectives, assumptions, fitting, interpretation, regularization, and residual diagnostics.
## Classification
Use logistic and other classifiers, probabilistic outputs, thresholds, and calibrated decisions.
## Trees & Ensemble Models
Understand decision trees, bagging, random forests, gradient boosting, feature importance, and tuning.
## Unsupervised Learning
Apply clustering, dimensionality reduction, density methods, and anomaly detection appropriately.
## Model Evaluation
Choose splits, cross-validation, baselines, metrics, confidence, thresholding, and error analysis.
## Overfitting & Regularization
Reason about bias and variance and use penalties, validation, early stopping, augmentation, and more data.
## Optimization
Cover losses, gradient descent variants, learning rates, convexity, local behavior, and convergence.
## Neural Networks
Understand layers, activations, backpropagation, initialization, normalization, regularization, and training.
## Natural Language Processing
Cover tokenization, representations, embeddings, sequence models, attention, transformers, and evaluation.
## Computer Vision
Cover image tensors, convolution, augmentation, classification, detection, segmentation, and transfer learning.
## ML Deployment & MLOps
Version models and data, serve predictions, test pipelines, monitor drift, and retrain safely.
## Responsible ML
Evaluate fairness, bias, explainability, privacy, safety, misuse, and human oversight.
